SCHENGEN VISA COVER LETTER TEMPLATE
From flypassholidays.co.uk/guides/schengen-visa-cover-letter
Copy the structure, not the sentences. Replace every [bracket] with your own
details, delete these top lines, and sign it. One page is enough.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

[Your full name]
[Your address in the UK]
[Phone] - [Email]
[Date]

To the Visa Officer
Consulate General of [country], London

Application for a short-stay Schengen visa, [tourism / business / visiting family]

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am applying for a short-stay Schengen visa to travel to [country] from
[entry date] to [exit date]. I am a [nationality] national, born [date of
birth], passport number [number], and I have lived in the United Kingdom
since [year]. I currently hold [your UK status], valid until [expiry date].

[Country] is my main destination and I will spend all [number] nights there.
I will fly from [UK airport] to [destination airport] on [entry date] and
return on [exit date]. I will stay at [hotel name and address] for the full
period. My flight reservation and hotel confirmation are enclosed.

The purpose of my trip is [purpose]. I am travelling [alone / with my
partner / with my family] and intend to spend the time in [cities or areas].

I am employed as a [job title] at [employer] in [city], where I have worked
since [start date]. My employer has approved annual leave from [leave start]
to [leave end] and a letter confirming this is enclosed. I am funding the
trip from my own savings and have enclosed bank statements for the past six
months.

I will return to the United Kingdom on [exit date] to resume work on
[return-to-work date]. My employment, my home at the address above and my
[status] here are all in the UK.

I have enclosed the following documents:
[short list of enclosed documents]

I would be grateful if you would consider my application. Please contact me
if anything further is needed.

Yours faithfully,
[Signature]
[Printed name]

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Before you submit, check three things:
1. The dates in this letter match your flight reservation, your employer's
   letter and your bookings exactly.
2. Your main destination is the country where you spend the most nights.
   If nights are split equally, it is the country you enter first.
3. Nothing in the letter claims anything your enclosed documents cannot show.
