France Has Changed How Visa Appointments Work

Since 1 August 2026 TLScontact allocates French short-stay appointments from a queue: no calendar, a 24-hour confirmation window, and a file that has to be ready before the date lands.

ETIAS Arrives in October. Most of Our Clients Do Not Need It
